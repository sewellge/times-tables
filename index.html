How to play (tap to expand) Tap two tiles where one is the other multiplied by the times table shown above (e.g. with ×5: 3 and 15, or 6 and 30). They must be in the same row, column, or a straight diagonal, with no other tile blocking the path between them — cleared spaces AND hearts don't block, so a match can connect straight through a heart. Do that and the heart pops for +10 (watch it float to the top of the board) plus a shuffle token, for each heart crossed. The board wraps around on every edge (left-right and top-bottom, including diagonals). Clear every number in a row to pop its hearts for bonus points. Shuffling the remaining tiles costs one shuffle token — no tokens, no shuffle — and tokens reset whenever a new board is generated. Pressing New Board means answering 5 random times-table questions correctly first (one mistake just re-asks the question, but a second mistake restarts the whole set of 5), then spinning a wheel to pick the next table — no skipping either step. Out of shuffle tokens? The same 5-question quiz earns you one shuffle. After a match, everything above the gap — numbers and hearts alike — falls down to fill it. Every so often a match triggers a quick bonus question about that exact fact (sometimes flipped, sometimes as division) — get it right for extra points.
